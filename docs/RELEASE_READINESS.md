## Physical-device test cases

- Secure Enclave required/preferred policy and non-exportability.
- User-presence prompt wording, cancellation, lockout and background transitions.
- Keychain access while locked, after restart and after app reinstall.
- Camera permission denial, malformed QR, oversized QR and repeated QR handling.
- Dynamic Type largest sizes, VoiceOver order, increased contrast and reduced motion.
- Offline vault access, network loss during VP/VCI, deferred issuance and retry.
- Screenshot/background privacy and sensitive clipboard checks.
